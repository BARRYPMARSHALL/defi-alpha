# Retail DeFi Yield-Tracking UX Research — 2026 Findings

Research method: ~40 web searches across Reddit mirrors (r/defi, r/CryptoCurrency), exchange news desks, VC reports (a16z, IOSG, Reown/Nansen), product forums (Compound, Sanctum, Moonwell, Starknet), and 2025-26 surveys. Every claim is cited.

## 1. Top 10 frustrations of retail yield farmers (with evidence)

1. **Gas fees price small players out.** High gas fees are "literally killing DeFi adoption," with user-acquisition costs math showing retail can't profitably farm small positions ([CoinSpectator, Jan 2026](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2026/01/05/high-gas-fees-are-literally-killing-defi-adoption-ran-the-numbers-on-user-acquisition-costs/)); Ethereum gas spikes as traders rotate to stable yields ([USD Mirror](https://usdmirror.com/ethereum-gas-spikes-as-defi-traders-rotate-to-stable-yields/)).
2. **APY numbers are misleading.** Blended/"stacked" APYs combine emission rewards + fees in ways that mislead retail ([CoinSpectator, Mar 2026](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2026/03/23/yield-stacking-in-defi-explained-why-that-blended-apy-number-is-misleading-and-how-to-read-it/)). "Yield porn" screenshots drive irrational capital allocation into AMM pools ([Moltbook Observatory](https://moltbook-observatory.sushant.info.np/posts/acc3dfbb-537f-40d2-bc73-f70fba229800)).
3. **Impermanent loss (IL) destroys "passive income".** Half of Uniswap LPs are losing money ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/half-of-uniswap-liquidity-providers-are-losing-money)); IL is −25.5% at a 5x price move ([QuantDecoded](https://quantdecoded.com/en/defi-automated-market-makers-uniswap-impermanent-loss)).
4. **Reward-token inflation/dilution.** Farm tokens get dumped; a GAIN token supply shock crashed price −90% and sparked community outrage ([Gate](https://data.gatenft.io/zh-tw/news/detail/14010842)); tokenomics that favor new LPs dilute existing farmers ([Laika AI](https://laikalabs.ai/app/saved-stories)); "liquidity mining mercenaries" extract value ([ChainScoreLabs](https://chainscorelabs.com/blog/tokenomics-design-mechanics-and-incentives/quantitative-token-modeling/the-hidden-cost-of-liquidity-mining-mercenaries)).
5. **Scams/rug pulls at industrial scale.** 89% of DEX pools studied were exploited in pump-and-dump scams ([CoinMarketCap study](https://coinmarketcap.com/community/pt-br/articles/679b78e6cb7c893c5b60a9f7/)); AI-driven impersonation scams caused ~$17B in 2025 losses ([Chainalysis via SignalPlus](https://t.signalplus.com/crypto-news/detail/impersonation-crypto-scams-ai-chainalysis-2025?lang=zh-TW)); rug-pull case studies are catalogued quarterly ([Quadriga Initiative](https://quadrigainitiative.com/casestudy/credixcompromisedadminkeyinsiderexploitrugpull.php)).
6. **Complex onboarding: 90% of connected wallets never transact** ([ChainAware](https://chainaware.ai/blog/defi-onboarding-in-2026-why-90-of-connected-wallets-never-transact/)); "Connect Wallet" is called the worst first step in DeFi ([ChainScoreLabs](https://chainscorelabs.com/blog/the-state-of-web3-education-and-onboarding/defi-onboarding-frameworks/why-connect-wallet-is-the-worst-first-step-in-defi)).
7. **Fragmentation across chains/wallets.** Most crypto users now manage multiple wallets (2+ on average in US/UK), making portfolio tracking hard ([Reown/Nansen 2025 Onchain UX Report via The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/research-and-opinion/majority-of-crypto-users-now-manage-multiple-wallets-reown-report) and [CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/active-crypto-users-in-us-uk-manage-more-than-two-mobile-wallets-study/)).
8. **Rewards dry up and APRs decay silently.** Yield farms struggle as incentives end ([USD Mirror](https://usdmirror.com/defi-yield-farms-struggle-as-rewards-dry-up/)); APY measurement itself is inconsistent across protocols ([DefiLlama yield-server issue #6](https://github.com/DefiLlama/yield-server/issues/6)).
9. **Retail is squeezed out by whales and exploits.** "The Playground of Whales" — retail investors flee as whales dominate and exploit fallout (KelpDAO etc.) spreads ([Panews](https://www.panewslab.com/en/articles/6b7b5f30-fdd0-44cd-abc5-150df71a5bed), [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/why-retail-investors-are-leaving-defi-amid-rising-exploitation)).
10. **Knowledge gap.** Surveys show growing crypto *awareness* but limited *understanding* of how products work ([BusinessMirror survey, Feb 2025](https://businessmirror.com.ph/2025/02/05/survey-reveals-growing-global-crypto-awareness-but-limited-understanding/)).

## 2. Most-requested dashboard/tracker features (with evidence)

- **Realistic APY history (weekly/monthly averages, not live APR)** — requested on the Compound forum ([comp.xyz proposal](https://www.comp.xyz/t/add-weekly-and-monthly-average-apr-to-frontend-dashboard/5958/5)) and by DefiLlama users ([measurement issue](https://github.com/DefiLlama/yield-server/issues/6)).
- **Yield profit visualization** — what you actually earned vs. what you'd have earned holding ([Sanctum research proposal](https://research.sanctum.so/t/proposal-yield-profit-visualization/164/11)).
- **IL calculator integrated into tracking** — "shows if fees cover IL" ([ILCalc on Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/ilcalc); [LP loss vs holding tool](https://onetradejournal.com/tools/impermanent-loss-calculator)).
- **Unified cross-chain view of staking/DeFi/NFT positions** ([OMS](https://www.omsltd.net/why-staking-rewards-defi-positions-and-nft-holdings-need-one-unified-view-and-how-to-build-it/); [Starknet community dashboard request](https://community.starknet.io/t/create-dashboard-for-tracking-your-defi-portfolio-request/63387/23)).
- **P&L upgrades (realized vs unrealized)** ([CoinStats P&L upgrade](https://www.htx.com/feed/community/5165595/); [ChainChat real-time analytics issue](https://github.com/gboigwe/ChainChat/issues/26)).
- **Risk/liquidation awareness built into the dashboard** ([Moonwell embedding yield tracker in protocol UI](https://forum.moonwell.fi/t/mip-embedding-yecho-yield-tracker-as-native-analytics-in-moonwell-ui/2062)).

## 3. Why retail abandons DeFi tools

- Gas fees and acquisition-cost math ([CoinSpectator](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2026/01/05/high-gas-fees-are-literally-killing-defi-adoption-ran-the-numbers-on-user-acquisition-costs/)), exploitation and rug pulls ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/why-retail-investors-are-leaving-defi-amid-rising-exploitation)), onboarding drop-off ([ChainAware](https://chainaware.ai/blog/defi-onboarding-in-2026-why-90-of-connected-wallets-never-transact/)), and a general "crypto UX crisis" that keeps billions out ([Bitget/Cointelegraph opinion](https://www.bitget.site/news/detail/12560605119544)). IL losses ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/half-of-uniswap-liquidity-providers-are-losing-money)) and dried-up rewards ([USD Mirror](https://usdmirror.com/defi-yield-farms-struggle-as-rewards-dry-up/)) complete the picture — users leave when the tool doesn't warn them about these.

## 5. What makes a yield tool feel premium / worth $10–20/mo

- **Market precedent:** CoinStats charges **from $15.99/mo** for premium/AI plans ([ComparEdge pricing](https://comparedge.com/tools/coinstats/pricing)), and premium tracker tiers are standard ([ComparEdge tracker pricing roundup](https://comparedge.com/best/crypto-portfolio-trackers)).
- **AI assistance has real demand:** CoinGecko's survey found **87% of crypto users willing to let AI manage part of their assets (some ≥$10K)** ([Bitget/CoinGecko](https://www.bitget.site/news/detail/12560604718870)) — but 19% of consumers report losses from bad AI financial advice ([AccountingToday](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/19-lost-100-or-more-on-bad-ai-financial-advice)), so an *advisory* AI (explain, warn, simulate) beats a *decision-making* AI.
- **Risk intelligence is being productized for retail** — Credora is democratizing risk ratings for individual DeFi users ([The AI Journal](https://aijourn.com/credora-network-democratizes-risk-intelligence-opening-platform-to-individual-defi-users/)); former OKX legal execs launched a DeFi risk-rating service ([CoinDesk, Mar 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/03/11/former-okx-legal-execs-unveil-defi-connectivity-risk-rating-service)); ETHPrague 2026 framed risk ratings as "the missing piece for DeFi mass adoption" ([talk](https://cfp.ducttape.events/ethprague-2026/talk/JTLJFY/)).
- **Premium = trust + proactivity:** liquidation/risk alerts (EPNS x AAVE, [Push](https://comms.push.org/blog/epns-x-aave-protection/); [DeFi.org notifications](https://defi.org/notifications/); [DeFi Saver notify](https://help.defisaver.com/features/notify/use-case.md)), accurate APY history, and IL-aware P&L — the things free dashboards do badly.

## 6. Demand evidence for specific features

- **IL calculator:** strong demand (Product Hunt launches, [ILCalc](https://www.producthunt.com/products/ilcalc), dedicated tools) because IL is the #1 misunderstood cost.
- **Portfolio tracking:** the category itself is proof — Zerion/DeBank/Zapper/CoinStats comparisons are a content genre ([CleanSky](https://cleansky.io/compare/), [Chainglance](https://chainglance.com/blog/7-best-defi-portfolio-trackers-in-2026/)); CoinStats advertises DeFi position detection across 120+ chains.
- **Alerts/notifications:** entire products exist to fill the gap — DeFi.org, Push/EPNS, DeFi Saver, DefiMonitor liquidation alerts ([DefiMonitor](https://defi-monitor.com/blog/defi-liquidation-alerts-stop-losing-money-to-preventable-liquidations/)); exchanges keep adding alert features ([Changelly](https://changelly.com/blog/changelly-introduces-crypto-alerts-to-help-you-stay-ahead-of-market-swings/)).
- **APY-change alerts:** implied by the Compound/DefiLlama measurement complaints — users can't trust live APR, so they need change alerts + history.
- **Protocol risk scores:** high and growing demand (Credora, OKX execs' service, ETHPrague session — see §5).
- **AI chat advisor:** high openness (87% CoinGecko) but trust-sensitive (19% losses stat); coaching platforms are emerging ([SparkyFi AI coaching](https://bostontechnologyreview.com/article/917616985-sparkyfi-launches-ai-powered-defi-coaching-platform-designed-to-close-the-gap-between-defi-s-promise-and-its-reality-for-everyday-investors)).
- **Wallet connect:** a bottleneck, not a feature — 90% connect-never-transact ([ChainAware](https://chainaware.ai/blog/defi-onboarding-in-2026-why-90-of-connected-wallets-never-transact/)).
- **Social features:** copy trading is a $2.6B market growing fast ([CoinSpectator/Versus Trade](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2025/11/26/versus-trade-taps-into-a-2-6b-global-copy-trading-market-on-the-rise/)).
- **Education:** "Earn & Learn" is a roadmap item at DeFi products ([Ethos DeFi](https://www.ethosdefi.com/ethos-defi-v6-roadmap-series-earn-learn-and-ethos-signal)); 21 crypto firms pushed DeFi courses into US colleges ([CMC](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/uk/article/21-crypto-firms-push-us-colleges-to-add-defi-courses)).

## Key takeaways for a retail yield tool
1. **Trust features** (real APY history, IL-aware P&L, risk scores) are the highest-urgency gap — they address frustrations #2, #3, #4 and the scam fear (#5).
2. **Mobile-first is non-negotiable** — the growth user base is phone-only, emerging-market, Gen Z.
3. **$10–20/mo is a proven price band** (CoinStats $15.99/mo), but only if premium = risk intelligence + alerts + accurate analytics, not just more charts.
4. **AI should advise, not decide** — demand is high, but 19% loss rates on AI advice mean an explainable advisor wins.
5. **Notifications are the cheapest retention lever** — the infrastructure exists (Push/EPNS) and users demonstrably pay for liquidation/APY alerts.

**Methodology notes / caveats:** Reddit thread content was accessed via mirrors (reddit.fsky.io, traderforum.com) since native Reddit blocks scraping; the "[Yield Farming Reddit: What Actually Works in 2026](https://theledgermind.com/yield-farming-reddit/)" aggregation was a useful secondary source but its data quality couldn't be independently verified. The 89% DEX-pool-scam figure and 90% connect-never-transact figure come from vendor-published studies (CoinMarketCap guest post, ChainAware) — directionally credible but not peer-reviewed. Willingness-to-pay is inferred from existing premium pricing ($15.99/mo CoinStats) plus AI-adoption surveys, not from a dedicated subscription survey, which would be the natural next research step.
